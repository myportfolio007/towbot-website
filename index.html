How it works

Deploy. Attach. Manoeuvre. Recover.

A modular sequence designed around safe human authority, situational awareness and a defined operating envelope.

Deploy

Launch from shore, a floating terminal or support vessel and establish communications.

Approach

Navigate to the selected hull location using cameras, sensors and assisted control.

Attach

Secure the application-specific hull interface and verify holding condition before thrust.

Control thrust

Command magnitude and direction from the designated control station, with override and emergency stop.

Recover

Release in a controlled manner, recover the unit, inspect, charge and prepare for the next duty cycle.

Frequently asked

Straight answers about Towbot.

What is Towbot?

Towbot is a configurable family of detachable battery-electric manoeuvring units. A Towbot is intended to deploy to a compatible vessel or floating asset, attach temporarily below the waterline and provide directly commanded thrust without a towing line or permanent hull conversion.

Can Towbot replace a harbour tug?

Towbot is designed to replace or supplement selected tug-assist tasks where the vessel, manoeuvre, environment and approvals are suitable. It is not presented as a universal replacement for every towage operation.

Can Towbot operate autonomously?

Towbot is designed for supervised remote operation and autonomous-assisted functions such as approach, positioning and control support. A designated human operator retains command authority and can override or stop the system.

Is Towbot commercially available?

Tow-botic Systems is accepting application, pilot and project-development enquiries across the TB product range. Final configuration, delivery scope and operating envelope are established through application engineering, validation and the required approvals.
